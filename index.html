Meet Rema Caffè

Coffee belongs inside a life well enjoyed.

Rema Caffè is built around pleasure in the everyday coffee moment — a quiet morning, a café-style treat, a conversation, or a small break from the routine. EUspresso brings that European coffee experience to U.S. customers and retail partners.

  • Crafted in Plovdiv, Bulgaria
  • Roasted, ground and packaged at origin
  • Produced using renewable solar energy
  • Imported and supported in the U.S. by EUspresso

FAQ

Good to know.

When will online ordering be available?

EUspresso is preparing the U.S. launch. Join the launch list for availability and ordering updates.

Which machines are compatible?

The range includes capsules for Nespresso Original compatible machines and drinks for Dolce Gusto compatible machines.

Where is Rema Caffè made?

Rema Caffè products are made in Plovdiv, Bulgaria, and imported to the United States by EUspresso.

Do you offer wholesale?

Yes. We are building relationships with retailers, specialty markets, online coffee sellers and regional wholesale partners.
